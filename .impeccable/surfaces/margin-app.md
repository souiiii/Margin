# Margin application

## Direction contract
THESIS: Reading, collecting, practice and retention form one quiet personal tutor. The original Mochi-inspired identity is supplied by the user; no visual alternatives are needed.
OWN-WORLD: Soft cool canvas, teal ink and actions, cyan bookmarks, peach selections. Anonymous Pro signature headings; Literata reading; Inter dense controls. Rounded geometry is reserved for empty states and the paper/bookmark motif.
STORY: Add your own reading, notice useful language, keep it with its context, then recall and use it. Real learning data replaces empty states; no preloaded articles, arbitrary statistics, or fixed grammar content.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 218px sidebar, 62px toolbar, compact heading, one contextual next action. Today contains a restrained next-step surface, daily discovery action, and real reading/bank lists. Reader uses a narrow Literata column and notes rail, collapsing below on mobile. Practice focuses on one exercise or recall at a time.
FORM: User-pinned Mochi/Reader references; code-led Operate and Read. No concept roll ran and no seed was issued. The initial context launcher failed because its engine cache was unavailable; the existing user-pinned visual brief governed implementation. No decision comp, catalog board, or QUALITY BAR card was selected. This is a disclosed workflow exception, not a validated unavailable-roll result. Signature interaction: selecting prose reveals a compact toolbar; collection leaves a peach mark and feedback. Motion is 160ms, state-driven, reduced-motion aware.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
